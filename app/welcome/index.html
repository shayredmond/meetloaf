Welcome to MeetLoaf

A standalone Google Meet app — its own window, global hotkeys, and link routing from your browser. Quick tour?

Global shortcuts

Mute, toggle your camera, raise your hand or leave — from any app, even when MeetLoaf isn’t focused. Open Settings (⌘ ,), click a shortcut to record it, then press the combo you want. Each one has a Global switch, in case you’d rather it only fired inside MeetLoaf.

Windows that stay out of the way

Settings → Window sets how MeetLoaf sits while you’re in a call:

  • Standard — behaves like any other window.
  • Always on top — floats above other apps, in a call only.
  • Pop out presentations — shares get their own window.

Meet links open here

Install the browser extension from Settings → Routing and meet.google.com links open in MeetLoaf instead of a tab. Firefox takes one click; Chrome and Edge need developer mode, and the panel walks you through it. Pair it with Velja to catch links from Slack, Mail and Calendar too.

You’re all set

Press ⌘ , any time to open Settings. That’s where the rest lives: which screen each window opens on, a Home Assistant automation when you join, and a button to replay this tour.
