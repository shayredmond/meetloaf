Welcome to MeetLoaf

A standalone Google Meet app for macOS — its own window, global hotkeys, and a mini-mode floating panel. Quick tour?

Global shortcuts

Mute, toggle your camera, or raise your hand from any app — even when MeetLoaf isn't focused. Configure them in Settings (⌘ ,) by clicking a shortcut field and pressing the combo you want.

Three window modes

Pick how MeetLoaf behaves during a call:

  • Standard — like a normal window.
  • Always on top — floats above other apps during a call.
  • Mini mode — shrinks to a small floating panel when MeetLoaf is covered or hidden.

Mini mode

A live preview of your meeting in the corner of your screen with hover controls for mute and camera. The border turns green when you're unmuted and red when muted, so you can glance and know.

You're all set

Press ⌘ , any time to open Settings — that's where you'll find shortcuts, window modes, and a button to revisit this tour.
