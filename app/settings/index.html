Link routing

When you click a meet.google.com link, MeetLoaf can intercept it instead of opening Meet in your browser. The Firefox extension handles links inside Firefox; pair it with Velja for links from Slack, Mail, Calendar, etc.

  1. Click Open extensions — your browser opens chrome://extensions.
  2. Toggle Developer mode (top-right of the page).
  3. Click Show folder here — Finder opens with the extension folder selected.
  4. Drag the extension-chrome folder onto the extensions page (or use Load unpacked and select it).

Home Assistant

Fire a Home Assistant automation when you join a meeting, and another when you leave. By default “join” means reaching the waiting room, so a camera or lighting scene is already up while you pick your devices.

Create an HA automation with a Webhook trigger and paste its ID above. The POST body carries event, meeting_code, url and timestamp — read them as {{ trigger.json.event }}.
The service follows the entity's domain: automation → trigger, script/scene → turn on, toggles like input_boolean turn on to join and off to leave.
